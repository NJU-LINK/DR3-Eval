01 / What is evaluated

Real research is not a clean prompt.

DR³-Eval brings authentic user materials, a controlled evidence space, and noisy retrieval conditions into one reproducible evaluation environment.

01—03

Three ingredients that make the task real.

Every task combines genuine research intent with the evidence conditions that agents face outside a benchmark sandbox.

Authentic user filesMultimodal, multi-file materials supplied by real users.
Per-case sandboxSupportive documents, distractors, and noise in a fixed corpus.
Reverse constructionQueries are anchored to verified evidence to reduce ambiguity.
Repeatable protocolStable retrieval space enables fair system comparisons.
Five dimensions

Measure retrieval and reporting separately.

A report can look complete while missing evidence or making unsupported claims. The metric suite exposes that gap.

03 / Diagnostic analysis

Failure modes become visible.

The updated paper makes the benchmark’s central message concrete: extra context adds noise, and instruction compliance is not factual reliability.

04 / From evidence to score

A closed loop for trustworthy evaluation.

The benchmark preserves the complexity of deep research while keeping every score traceable to a fixed evidence environment.

STAGE 01

Ground in real materials

Start with authentic user-provided files and the research needs behind them.

STAGE 02

Construct a sandbox

Build a controlled corpus with evidence, distractions, and realistic noise.

STAGE 03

Audit the report

Score recall, citations, facts, instruction following, and analytical depth.

Evaluator–human alignment

On a 50-report study, automated evaluation closely follows expert assessment: Pearson r = 0.88, Spearman ρ = 0.83, and pairwise agreement = 0.89.
